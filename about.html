📖 Our Story

Kaise sab shuru hua

1

Problem dikha (2021)

Mumbai ke Dharavi market mein, hamare founder Arjun ne dekha ki bahut saare chhote business owners roz 2-3 ghante sirf register likhne mein barbaad karte hain. Stock miss hota tha, orders bhool jaate the, customers frustrated hote the. Excel bhi bahuts complicated tha.

2

Idea aaya (2022)

"Agar log itna WhatsApp use kar sakte hain, toh smart Voice AI se godown kyon nahi manage kar sakte?" — yeh ek sawal Logistey bana. Pehla prototype 3 Kirana stores ne try kiya, aur feedback overwhelming tha: "Yeh kaam karta hai!"

3

Grow kiya (2023-24)

Mumbai se Bhiwandi, Pune, Nagpur, Surat — 1,200 se zyada business owners ne adopt kiya. Hamare users ne average mein 2 ghante roz bachaye aur 35% fewer order errors report kiye. Logistey apna vision prove kar chuka hai.

4

Future hai bada (2025+)

Hum aage aur cities, languages, aur industry verticals mein expand kar rahe hain. Vision simple hai: India ke har chhote business owner ke haath mein ek smart AI assistant. "Har dukaan ko smart banana" — yeh mission abhi bhi jaari hai.
